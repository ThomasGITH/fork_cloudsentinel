Models and preprocessing
- CGNN-MHSA-AR 1.0.0: MinMaxScaler fitted only on training data; lookback 100;
  five epochs; batch size 256; validation fraction 0.1; CUDA off; feature
  importance and TensorBoard logging off. Its first 100 evaluation observations
  are warm-up and are excluded from metric calculation.
- Isolation Forest 1.0.0: persisted StandardScaler + IsolationForest pipeline;
  100 estimators, contamination=auto, max_samples=auto, random_state=42,
  n_jobs=1; no warm-up.
- Local Outlier Factor 1.0.1 (external plugin): persisted StandardScaler +
  novelty-enabled LOF pipeline; n_neighbors=20, Minkowski p=2,
  contamination=auto; no warm-up.
- Binary label/prediction semantics: 0=normal, 1=anomaly.
- Comparison metrics: precision=TP/(TP+FP), recall=TP/(TP+FN), and harmonic-mean F1;
  zero denominators are represented as 0.0 by the current implementation.
