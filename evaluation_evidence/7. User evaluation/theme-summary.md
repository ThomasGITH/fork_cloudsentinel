# Common feedback from the three experts

Some feedback that was mentioned every session:

- **Separate screens were easier to understand than the whole process.** They pretty much understood each screen on themselves, but they didnt always know what part to go next.

- **Live Monitoring and RCA-fetures were seen as useful.** They each mentioned this as the most useful.

- **The workflow seemed more useful for research than daily operations.** Te features of collecting datasets, training detectors, and comparing models were more recognized as useful for research controlled experiments rather than for commercial use, even though they seemed to like the features. A company operational team would want a more 'out of the box' experience.

- **Setup and technical terms still need work.** They wanted less manual configuration and clearer explanations about all the parameters, metrics, and also the robustness results.
